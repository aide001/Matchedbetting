Turn bookmaker free bets into real profit

Matched betting covers every outcome of a bet, so the result of the game doesn't decide whether you make money. Use the calculator to get the right lay stake, then track every offer you complete.

How it works

1

Back at the bookmaker

Place a qualifying bet on an outcome, for example "Arsenal to win", to unlock a free bet offer.

2

Lay at the exchange

Bet against the same outcome on a betting exchange. The calculator tells you how much to lay so both sides cancel out.

3

Extract the free bet

Repeat with the free bet. Because you don't lose its stake, you lock in most of its value as profit, whatever the result.

Tools

Oddsmatcher

Compares live UK bookmaker prices with Betfair, Smarkets and Matchbook lay odds, then ranks every pair by rating. Use it for qualifying bets or free bets.

Matched betting calculator

Works out lay stakes for qualifying bets and free bets, both stake-not-returned and stake-returned, including exchange commission. Underlay and overlay with the slider.

Profit tracker

Log each bet and see your running profit by bookmaker and by month. Data stays in your browser, and you can export it to CSV.
Play it safe. Matched betting is low risk only when you follow the process carefully. Double-check odds before placing bets, keep enough funds in your exchange to cover liabilities, and never bet money you can't afford to lose.
