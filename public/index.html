How it works

1

Back at the bookmaker

Place a qualifying bet on an outcome, for example "Arsenal to win", to unlock a free bet offer.

2

Lay at the exchange

Bet against the same outcome on a betting exchange. The calculator tells you how much to lay so both sides cancel out.

3

Extract the free bet

Repeat with the free bet. Because you don't lose its stake, you lock in most of its value as profit, whatever the result.
