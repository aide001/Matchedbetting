How to complete a sign-up offer
  1. Read the offer terms: minimum odds, qualifying stake, eligible markets and when the free bet arrives.
  2. Find a close match in the oddsmatcher using the Normal rating, then place the qualifying bet and lay it straight away.
  3. When the free bet lands, switch the oddsmatcher to SNR, pick a high rating at longer odds, and back and lay it.
  4. Log both bets in the profit tracker and mark the offer as done here.
